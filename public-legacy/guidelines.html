Community Guidelines

Unigram is an unofficial, student-run platform for Chaudhary Ranbir Singh University (CRSU), Jind. These guidelines exist to make it a safe, respectful, and genuinely useful space for everyone on campus.

🤝 Be Respectful

  • Treat every student, faculty member, and staff person with basic respect — even when you disagree.
  • No personal attacks, bullying, harassment, or targeted ridicule of individuals or groups.
  • Avoid language that demeans people based on gender, caste, religion, region, or background.
  • Criticism of ideas is welcome. Attacks on people are not.

🎭 Anonymous Feed Rules

  • Anonymity is a privilege, not a shield. Use it responsibly.
  • Posting someone's name or identifying details to harass them anonymously is a bannable offense.
  • Admins can reveal your identity on the anonymous feed if a post violates community rules.
  • The same rules that apply to the named feed apply to the anonymous feed.

🚫 Prohibited Content

  • No hate speech, slurs, or content that dehumanizes individuals or communities.
  • No sexual, explicit, or NSFW content of any kind.
  • No sharing of exam paper leaks, academic dishonesty material, or plagiarized content.
  • No spam, chain messages, referral solicitations, or self-promotion posts.
  • No misinformation presented as fact — especially about university events, exams, or faculty.
  • No content that could jeopardize someone's safety or expose private information (doxxing).

📄 Notes & PYQ Uploads

  • Only upload materials you own or have permission to share.
  • Do not upload content from paid courses, copyrighted books, or restricted university materials.
  • Label uploads accurately: wrong subject/semester labels are unhelpful and will be flagged.

💬 Direct Messages

  • DMs are private, but violations reported by recipients are still subject to review.
  • Unsolicited explicit content or harassment via DM is a bannable offense.
  • Block and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable — admins take reports seriously.

⚠️ Enforcement

  • Moderators may remove any post or comment that violates these guidelines without prior notice.
  • Serious or repeated violations will result in a suspension or permanent ban.
  • Banned users lose access to all features, including reading the feed.
  • Appeal a moderation decision by contacting a super-admin through an active account.
Not a university-official platform. Unigram is independently run by students and is not affiliated with CRSU administration. Disputes of a serious nature (harassment, threats, etc.) should also be reported to the university.
