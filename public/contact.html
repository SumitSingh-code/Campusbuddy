Grievance Redressal

Unigram operates as an intermediary under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. We maintain a dedicated grievance mechanism to handle complaints about content, privacy, and account issues.

2. IT Rules 2021 Compliance

  • Unigram operates as a social media intermediary under the IT Rules 2021 and maintains this grievance mechanism in accordance with Rule 4(2).
  • We are committed to acknowledging complaints within 24 hours and resolving or responding to content-related complaints within 36 hours, as required.
  • For other matters (data, account), we target resolution within 7 business days.
  • We do not knowingly host content that is unlawful under Indian law. If we become aware of such content, we act promptly to remove it.

3. How to File a Complaint

  • Email grievance@unigram.app — this is the only official channel for formal grievance submission.
  • Use the subject line format: "Grievance: [Issue Type]"
  • Examples of subject lines:
    — "Grievance: Defamatory Post"
    — "Grievance: Privacy"
    — "Grievance: Account Issue"
    — "Grievance: Harassment"
    — "Grievance: Impersonation"
  • In the body of your email, include:
    — Your full name and roll number (required to verify you are a platform user)
    — A clear description of the issue
    — A link to the post or profile in question, or a screenshot, if applicable
    — Any relevant dates or context
  • Anonymous grievance submissions will be reviewed but we may be unable to provide a response without contact information.

5. Types of Complaints We Handle

  • Content complaints: Posts or messages that are defamatory, harassing, abusive, explicit, or otherwise illegal under Indian law. Provide a link or screenshot.
  • Privacy and data requests: Request to access, correct, or delete your personal data held by Unigram. See our Privacy Policy for your rights under the DPDP Act 2023.
  • Account issues: Reports of wrongful suspension or ban, account impersonation, or unauthorised access to your account.
  • Technical issues: Bugs or platform behaviour that exposes personal data, breaks safety features, or causes harm. Please describe the issue in detail.

6. Data Deletion Requests

  • To request deletion of your account and personal data, email grievance@unigram.app with the subject line: "Data Request: Delete Account".
  • Include your full name, roll number, and the email address or phone number associated with your account so we can identify you.
  • We will process the deletion within 30 days of receiving a valid request.
  • Upon deletion: your profile and personal identifiers are removed. Some content may be retained in anonymized form for moderation records as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • You will receive an email confirmation once deletion is complete.

7. Appealing a Decision

  • If you are unsatisfied with how a grievance was handled, you may submit an appeal.
  • Email grievance@unigram.app with the subject line: "Grievance Appeal: [Original ticket date]" (e.g., "Grievance Appeal: 2026-08-14").
  • In your appeal, reference the original complaint and explain why you believe the resolution was inadequate.
  • Appeals are reviewed by a senior member of the Unigram team and we will respond within 7 business days.
  • If you remain unsatisfied after the appeal, you may escalate to the relevant authority under Indian law, including the Grievance Appellate Committee (GAC) established under IT Rules 2021 (once operational for platforms of our scale).

8. Important Note

  • Unigram is a student-run, non-commercial platform maintained on a voluntary basis. We are not a 24/7 commercial operation.
  • While we are committed to the timelines above, occasional delays may occur during university exam periods or holidays. We appreciate your patience.
  • We take all reports seriously and review them in good faith. Frivolous or bad-faith complaints (e.g., attempting to get valid content removed through false reports) may result in action against the complainant's account.
  • For matters involving criminal activity, serious threats, or immediate physical danger, please contact local law enforcement directly. Do not wait for our response.
Not a university-official platform. Unigram is independently run by students and is not affiliated with CRSU administration. For serious matters involving harassment or threats, also report to the university's student grievance cell and, if necessary, to law enforcement.
